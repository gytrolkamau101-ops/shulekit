# How ShuleMoja protects school data

For Principals and Boards:

1. **Your data stays yours** — Student, fee, and exam records belong to the school. ShuleMoja (BigDataKE Technologies) hosts the platform; we do not sell school data.
2. **Access control** — Staff see only what their role allows (Principal, Deputy, Secretary, Teacher, Parent). Parents only see their linked children.
3. **Login security** — Accounts use email/password via Supabase Auth. Share passwords only within your school policy.
4. **Payments** — Platform subscription is paid via IntaSend STK. School fees stay with the school’s Till/Paybill; ShuleMoja does not collect parent fee money.
5. **Audit** — Important actions can be reviewed by Super Admin / Principal where audit logging is enabled.
6. **Backups & uptime** — Data is stored on managed cloud infrastructure (Supabase/PostgreSQL) with provider-level backups.
7. **Your rights** — A school may export or request deletion of its data by contacting BigDataKE support.

Contact: use the Contact details on the login page (set by Super Admin).
